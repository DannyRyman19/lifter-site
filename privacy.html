Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Lifter does not collect, store or transmit personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. We have no servers that receive your data.

What the app stores

Your workouts, plans, custom exercises, records, body weight entries and settings are saved on your devices only: on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch for workouts logged there. The iPhone and Watch apps pass this data directly between each other. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Lifter reads data from Apple Health to show your recovery and training load: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen, sleep, VO2 max, activity and energy, body measurements, protein and calories eaten, mindful minutes and workouts. It saves the strength workouts you log back to Apple Health. Health data is read and used on your device only. It is never sent off your device, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. You can change or withdraw access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

AI Coach

The coach builds workouts using Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone. What you type and the training history it uses stay on the device.

Purchases

Lifter Pro is sold through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and the subscription. The app only receives your subscription status from Apple and never sees your payment details. Apple’s handling of the purchase is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Exports and sharing

If you export your history or share a workout, the file or image goes only where you choose to send it, using the iOS share sheet.

Children

Lifter is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
