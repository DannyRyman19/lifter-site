Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone and Apple Watch only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, so deleting the app deletes your history. An iPhone backup includes it.

My Watch workout isn’t on my iPhone

Sets are saved on the Watch first and sent when the two can connect. Open Lifter on the iPhone with the Watch nearby and it will arrive.

The coach isn’t available

The coach uses Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone that supports it (iPhone 15 Pro or later) with Apple Intelligence switched on in Settings.

How do I restore Lifter Pro?

Open Settings in Lifter and tap Restore Purchases. Pro is tied to the Apple account you bought it with.

How do I manage or cancel Lifter Pro?

In your device’s App Store account settings, under Subscriptions. Your logged workouts stay on your device after it ends.
